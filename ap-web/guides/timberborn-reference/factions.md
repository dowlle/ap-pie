Timberborn Archipelago supports both factions, Folktails and Iron Teeth. Each gets its own blueprint pool, with the buildings only that faction has.

## Choosing a faction

Set **Faction** in your YAML to `folktails` (the default) or `iron_teeth`. With `random` in the YAML the seed picks one for you. Ask your host which one you got; if you start the wrong one, the mod tells you when you connect.

Then start a new colony with that faction. If the colony's faction doesn't match the seed, the mod refuses to connect and the Archipelago log says **Wrong faction!** Start a new colony with the right faction; the mismatched save stays blocked from reconnecting.

## Unlocking Iron Teeth

In the normal game you unlock Iron Teeth by reaching well-being 8 with Folktails. You don't need to: the mod unlocks every faction as soon as a colony loads with the mod enabled. If Iron Teeth is still locked on the new-game screen, load any colony once, go back to the main menu and start your Iron Teeth colony.

## Badwater, Explosives and Extract

Buildings that use Explosives or Extract (the Dynamite family, Tunnel, Detonator, banners, Memory, Agora, Detailer, Decontamination Pod, Advanced Breeding Pod, Grease Factory and others) need a badwater source in logic, plus the Explosives Factory or the Centrifuge.

For Folktails, the Badwater Pump is a tier 3 blueprint. For Iron Teeth, the Deep Badwater Pump is a tier 5 blueprint, following its building costs in the normal game, so Explosives and Extract come later. Nothing a goal needs depends on Iron Teeth badwater.

The Iron Teeth Dance Pit also needs the Metalsmith in logic.

The other 88 buildings are shared. Iron Teeth gets the Scavenger Flag for free in the normal game, so it is not an item for them.

## Early survival picks

With Force Early Items and Extra Early Survival on (both default) and population milestones on, each seed forces one extra early building per category into your first sphere:

- **Folktails:** one housing building (Mini Lodge, Double Lodge or Triple Lodge), one food building (Aquatic Farmhouse, Gristmill or Beehive) and one well-being building (Lido or Herbalist), from those your first tiers can build.
- **Iron Teeth:** one housing building (Rowhouse, Large Barrack or Large Rowhouse) and one well-being building (Double Shower, Scratcher or Swimming Pool). Iron Teeth has no early food building in its pool, so there is no food pick.

Buildings that belong to an active progressive chain are left out of the picks.
