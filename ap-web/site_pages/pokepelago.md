Catch Pokémon by remembering their names.

Poképelago is a Pokémon guessing game you play in your browser. Type a Pokémon's name to catch it and fill your Pokédex. There is no ROM or emulator to install.

Play standalone to see how many names you know, or join an Archipelago multiworld. In a multiworld, your catches can send items to other games, while items from other players unlock more Pokémon for you to catch.
