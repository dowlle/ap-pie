Timberborn Archipelago

Play Timberborn as an Archipelago randomizer. The buildings you would normally unlock with science are spread across the multiworld: you buy checks in the AP Shop, and other players' games send you the blueprints your colony needs.

Join a game in three steps

Joining a multiworld somebody else is hosting? This is all you need. The setup guide has the details.

  1. Install the mod

    From mod.io or Steam Workshop, or extract the zip into your Timberborn Mods folder. Then enable it in the Mods window.

    How to install

  2. Make your YAML

    Pick your faction, goal and options, and send the file to your host.

    Open the YAML Builder

  3. Connect to your room

    Start a colony with your faction, open the AP Shop from the bottom toolbar and enter the room details.

    How to connect

What changes in your game

Your beavers start with almost nothing. Every seed spreads the tech tree differently, so you build with what the multiworld gives you.

Science buys checks

Four shop paths with rising prices and tiers. You never see what a slot holds until you buy it.

Checks: the AP Shop and milestones
