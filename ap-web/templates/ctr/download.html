Hosting or generating?

Players joining somebody else's multiworld only need the client above. If you are generating the multiworld yourself, you also need the CTR APWorld for your Archipelago install, and the release bundle includes a documented template YAML. You can also build a YAML in the browser from the APWorld index.

  • Download the CTR APWorld ({{ stable.version }})
