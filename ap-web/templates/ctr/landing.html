Crash Team Racing Archipelago

Play the original Crash Team Racing as an Archipelago randomizer. Your races can unlock progress in other players' games, and their games send the Trophies, Keys and Relics you need back to you.

Your first race in three steps

Joining a multiworld somebody else is hosting? This is all you need. The setup guide has the details.

  1. Download the game client

    Unzip it into a folder of its own. There is no emulator or separate connector to install.

    Downloads

  2. Add your disc image

    Copy the .bin of your North American disc into the assets folder. The game finds it by itself.

    How to add your disc

  3. Connect to your room

    In Options, then Connection, enter the room address, your slot name and the password if there is one.

    How to connect

What changes in your game

Adventure Mode becomes a new puzzle every seed. Instead of following the same route, you play with what your multiworld gives you.

Warp pads ask for new things

Every pad gets its own requirement each seed, shown above the pad.

Your progress comes from anywhere

Trophies, Keys, Relics and Gems are items, and another player's game may be holding yours.

Progression and kart upgrades
